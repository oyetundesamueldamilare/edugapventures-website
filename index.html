Who We Are

EDUGAP Ventures Limited is an organisation with its headquarters in Lagos, established to bridge the critical gap between education and funding.

We were founded on the conviction that every student — regardless of geography or financial standing — deserves access to quality educational institutions, and meaningful financial support. Our mandate spans from admission support to top schools, colleges, and technical training centres, to setting up reading rooms, data processing hubs, and hostels that create immersive learning environments.

Beyond infrastructure, we actively provide educational loans, and student career support to students both within and outside Nigeria. We also serve as a bridge between academicians, industry professionals, and government agencies — organising certified training programmes across online, distance, and in-person formats.

Our Mission

To provide value and quality service to students and instituitions by promoting, administering, and giving access to loans for educational advancement.

Our Vision

To be Nigeria's leading force in educational empowerment — utilizing emerging technologies to build centres of learning, make loans available, and advance trade, commerce, art, science, and technology.

IT Excellence

We carry on the business of Information Technology services — delivering software consulting, networking solutions, and digital infrastructure to institutions and enterprises.

Student-First Values

Every programme we run — from coaching classes to loan assistance to hostel accommodations — is designed to remove barriers and put students on the path to success.
